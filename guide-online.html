BIZGUIDE / PLANNING GUIDE

Plan your business website

A useful business website begins with a clear purpose. Decide what a visitor should learn and what action you want them to take.

Choose the job of the website

A resource site, a service introduction and an online store have different needs. Write down your primary audience and one main action before choosing hosting or adding features.

Plan your essential pages

Start with a home page, useful service or product information, an honest description of your business and appropriate privacy information. If you use affiliate links, explain that relationship where visitors encounter them.

Ask ownership and renewal questions

For a domain, clarify ownership, account access, renewal pricing and transfer options. For hosting, clarify support, recurring costs, content export and the features your site needs.

Review before launch

Check navigation, text readability, mobile layout and every destination link. Remove unfinished forms, unsupported claims and offers you cannot verify. Keep a plan for updating content as the business changes.
