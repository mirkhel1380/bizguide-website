Your business setup planning checklist

Before choosing a service, put your goals and unanswered questions in one place. A clear brief makes it easier to compare your needs with an actual service quote.

1. Describe your planned activity

Write a short description of what the business intends to do, who its customers will be and where it expects to operate. Keep uncertainties visible rather than filling them with assumptions.

2. Prepare questions for qualified advisers

List the legal, tax and reporting questions you need answered before ordering. Bring your circumstances to a qualified adviser and consult the relevant official authorities when checking requirements.

3. Define the help you want

Decide which tasks you want a provider to perform. Ask for a written description of inclusions, exclusions, required information and expected processing steps.

4. Create a renewal calendar

Record recurring services, renewal dates and cancellation procedures from the documents you actually accept. Keep a copy of your order confirmation and provider communications.
