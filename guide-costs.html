How to review a service quote

A headline price is a starting point. To understand an offer, read what it covers and when further charges may arise.

Separate the cost categories

Ask the provider to distinguish its service charge from any additional charges in your order. Request a breakdown of one-time costs, recurring services, optional extras and anything outside the quote.

Check the scope

Match each advertised feature with the order description. If a task matters to you, confirm whether it is included and who is responsible for completing it.

Review the renewal terms

Look for the renewal price, billing interval, cancellation method and notice period. Save the terms that apply when you order rather than relying on a summary alone.

Keep the decision grounded

Do not choose solely on an affiliate recommendation. A provider should answer your specific questions before you purchase. BizGuide does not publish unverified package prices or promise savings.

Your next step: request a written quote with inclusions, exclusions, renewal charges and cancellation terms.
