What does a registered agent do?

Reviewed October 14, 2026

Understand the role before choosing a service.

The core responsibility

A registered agent receives official and legal documents for the business. The agent must be located in the registration state.

What to ask before appointing one

  • Which state and entity does the service cover?
  • How are documents delivered to you?
  • What are the response and support arrangements?
  • Which address uses are permitted?
  • What renews, and how do you cancel or change providers?

Separate the role from package extras

Do not assume that general mail forwarding, a bank account, tax returns, business licences or unrestricted address use are included. Ask the provider to confirm any additional service in writing.
