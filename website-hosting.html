Website hosting

Prepare a home for your website. Review hosting needs, support and renewal costs.

Choose around your website’s needs

Hosting serves the content of your website. Start by listing what you plan to publish, how you will update it and who will maintain it. A simple informational site and a store may need different tools; confirm the plan supports the features you actually intend to use.

Check what is included

Ask about backups, restoration, account access and support. Confirm whether domain registration and business email are separate purchases. Keep the first payment, renewal amount and billing interval visible in your comparison rather than relying on an introductory offer alone.

Questions worth asking

  • What does the hosting plan include?
  • How is support provided?
  • What are the renewal and cancellation terms?
  • How can website content be moved or exported?

Plan for a future handover

Before ordering, ask how you can export your content and who has permission to publish changes. Keep an inventory of the accounts needed to manage the website. Use our website access checklist to prepare the handover questions.

What happens next?

Review the provider’s current service information, confirm the scope and terms, then complete any purchase on the provider’s own website. BizGuide does not take payments or submit registration orders.
