How to prepare for a U.S. LLC.

Reviewed October 14, 2026

A practical sequence for planning a filing, with decisions to confirm before you order.

Understand the structure

An LLC is formed under state law. Ownership and federal tax classification are separate questions; tax treatment depends on membership and elections.

1. Review your location

Check your formation state and where you conduct business. Activity in another state may require foreign qualification.

2. Prepare the filing

Confirm the name, address, ownership or management details and registered agent. States determine the required formation document and filing fee.

3. Document how the business operates

An operating agreement records internal decision-making and member responsibilities. Check state requirements and seek help for your circumstances.

4. Plan beyond formation

Review tax IDs, applicable licences, banking and recurring obligations with the relevant authorities.

Your preparation checklist

  • Write down the business activity and operating locations.
  • Ask which information becomes public.
  • Request a written scope and cost breakdown.
  • Keep copies of accepted filings and order confirmations.
  • Record deadlines and responsible people.
A provider can help with a filing, but formation alone does not complete every business obligation. Never assume that a bank account, tax election or licence is included in an order.
