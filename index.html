Good questions

Before you get started.

Is BizGuide the service provider?

No. BizGuide is an independent educational resource. Registered Agents Inc. processes its own orders, payments, cancellations and support requests.

Where can I confirm prices and service details?

Confirm the current offer directly with Registered Agents Inc. Review the order scope, recurring charges, cancellation terms and any additional costs before purchasing.

Does BizGuide collect my business registration details?

No. This version has no registration, checkout or contact forms. Do not send identity documents or payment details to BizGuide.

Will an affiliate link change how I place an order?

You will order on the provider’s website. An active tagged affiliate link may track your referral and may earn BizGuide a commission.
