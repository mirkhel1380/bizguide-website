Frequently asked questions.

Reviewed October 14, 2026

Clear answers about this website and the next questions to ask your provider.

Is BizGuide an approved affiliate?

Our affiliate application has not yet been approved. No affiliate referral links are active. We are an independent resource featuring Registered Agents Inc.

Can BizGuide register my company?

No. BizGuide provides educational content. A provider processes its own service orders.

How long will formation take?

It depends on the state, filing route and order. Request a current estimate from the provider; BizGuide does not guarantee a processing time.

What is included in the formation price?

Check the current provider offer and written order scope. See our cost guide for the published pricing and questions to ask.

Does a registered agent handle all my business compliance?

Review the exact package. Do not assume that tax filings, licences or all deadlines are covered. Our registered agent guide explains what to ask.

Can I get a refund or cancel?

Provider purchases follow the terms accepted at checkout. Ask the provider about refunds, recurring billing and cancellation before ordering.

Does formation guarantee a bank account or immigration status?

BizGuide offers no such guarantee. Confirm banking eligibility and any immigration question separately with the relevant institution or qualified adviser.

How can I report a website problem?

Email contact@bizguide.website with the page address and a brief description.
