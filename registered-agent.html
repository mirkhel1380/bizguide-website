QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before you make a decision.

Do I need an agent in every state?

Requirements depend on where the entity is registered and authorized to do business. Check each state's rules.

Is the service billed every year?

Many registered agent services renew annually. Confirm fees and cancellation deadlines.

What happens if I switch providers?

The official state record may need updating. Ask the provider about change-of-agent filings and handoff steps.
