Understand setup and renewal costs.

Reviewed October 19, 2026

A service fee and the complete cost of running a business are different things.

Cost categoryWhat to confirm
Formation serviceThe provider’s filing assistance and package inclusions.
State filing feesThe amount payable to your selected state.
Registered agentFirst-year inclusion, later annual cost and cancellation terms.
Other obligationsReporting, licences, tax preparation and any additional services relevant to your business.
Domain and hostingInitial price, renewal price, billing interval and optional extras.

Published provider pricing

Registered Agents Inc. lists formation at $100 plus state fees, with one year of registered agent service included. Its standalone registered agent service is listed at $200 per year.

Checked October 19, 2026. These are provider-published prices, not a binding BizGuide quote. Confirm the applicable renewal and all order charges directly.

Before you accept a quote

  • Ask for an itemized first-year total.
  • Ask for the next renewal amount and date.
  • Check optional services and recurring billing.
  • Save the refund and cancellation terms.

BizGuide does not take payments or guarantee discounts.
